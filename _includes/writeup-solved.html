{%- comment -%} How a writeup was solved (param: writeup). Outputs nothing; sets three variables: writeup_solved team | solo | upsolve (used by the filter) writeup_solved_label short text for badges (team name, "Solo", "Upsolve") writeup_solved_long text for the metadata ("With team xSTF", …) Front matter `solved_as` wins. Without it: "team" if the CTF has a `team` in _data/ctfs.yml, otherwise "solo". {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _ws = include.writeup -%} {%- assign _ws_ctf = site.data.ctfs | where: "id", _ws.ctf | first -%} {%- if _ws.solved_as -%} {%- assign writeup_solved = _ws.solved_as | downcase | strip -%} {%- elsif _ws_ctf.team -%} {%- assign writeup_solved = "team" -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign writeup_solved = "solo" -%} {%- endif -%} {%- case writeup_solved -%} {%- when "team" -%} {%- assign writeup_solved_label = _ws_ctf.team | default: "Team" -%} {%- if _ws_ctf.team -%} {%- assign writeup_solved_long = "Playing for " | append: _ws_ctf.team -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign writeup_solved_long = "Playing for the team " -%} {%- endif -%} {%- when "solo" -%} {%- assign writeup_solved_label = "Solo" -%} {%- assign writeup_solved_long = "Solo" -%} {%- when "upsolve" -%} {%- assign writeup_solved_label = "Upsolve" -%} {%- assign writeup_solved_long = "Upsolve (solved after the CTF ended)" -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign writeup_solved_label = writeup_solved | capitalize -%} {%- assign writeup_solved_long = writeup_solved_label -%} {%- endcase -%}
